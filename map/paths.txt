## Source repo
> Everything the Factory is made of, edited by hand. install.sh copies or links it to where it is read; nothing runs from here. The Factory depends on six pi packages from repos of their own, github.com/bradley-mankoff/pi-beads, pi-links, pi-chains, pi-notices, pi-flows and pi-bash-timeout, which `pi install` puts in the pi folders; its two pi extensions of its own, project-manager/status-check.ts and project-manager/blocked-mark.ts, are loaded only by a Project Manager (fac-k10, fac-3mz.5, fac-qpv.8.1).
