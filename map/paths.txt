## Source repo
> Everything the Factory is made of, edited by hand. install.sh copies or links it to where it is read; nothing runs from here. The Factory depends on five pi packages from repos of their own, github.com/bradley-mankoff/pi-beads, pi-links, pi-chains, pi-notices and pi-flows, which `pi install` puts in the pi folders; it holds no pi extension itself (fac-qpv.8.1).
